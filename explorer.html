Tour Exploration

Explore Player Putting Performance

Use the interactive tool below to explore putting performance across individual PGA Tour tournaments.

How to use this interactive plot:

  • Hover over any point to inspect the specific tournament name, date, total putts, and average putts per round.
  • Click on a player’s name in the legend on the right to toggle them on or off.
  • Double-click on a player’s name to isolate only that golfer.
  • Click and drag on the chart area to zoom into any specific season or time window.

The dashed gray line represents the Tour-wide median of 28.75 putts per round. Notice how even perennial major contenders oscillate above and below the baseline from week to week.
